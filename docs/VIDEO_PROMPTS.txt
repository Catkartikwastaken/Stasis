Video Generation Prompts for STASIS Competition Website Assets
=====================================================================

Use these prompts with video/image generation tools (Runway ML, Pika, Sora,
Kling, Stable Video Diffusion, etc.) to create the three demo videos referenced
in docs/index.html.

Place the generated MP4 files in: docs/assets/

---

1. STASIS PATROL SCROLL
   Required filename: docs/assets/stasis-patrol-scroll.mp4

   A small four-wheeled Raspberry Pi rover with a USB webcam mounted on top slowly
   drives forward through an indoor forest-like demo space. The scene shows potted
   plants, artificial foliage, and a wooden floor. The camera perspective is the
   rover's first-person view (FPV). The rover moves methodically in a straight line
   scanning left and right with slight panning. Overlaid HUD elements show a faint
   crosshair, grid lines, and the text "FPV SOURCE // DIRECT FEED" and
   "[MONITORING ACTIVE]" in green monospace font. The lighting is dim indoor with
   warm side-lighting. No humans visible. The rover's movement is smooth and steady
   like a patrol route. Duration: 8-10 seconds. Resolution: 1920x1080. Style:
   cinematic, moody, tactical, blue-green color grade, slight film grain.

   Preferred tool: Runway Gen-3 or Kling with an image of the STASIS rover chassis
   as the starting frame.


2. STASIS DETECT SCROLL
   Required filename: docs/assets/stasis-detect-scroll.mp4

   Continuation of the patrol scene from the rover's FPV camera. As the rover enters
   a new area of the indoor forest demo, a bounding box appears around a person
   standing among the plants. The box glows amber/gold and a label reads
   "HUMAN [91% CONFIDENCE]" in monospace text above it. A second smaller bounding
   box in green appears over a plush animal toy (stand-in for wildlife), labeled
   "ANIMAL [86% CONFIDENCE]". The rover slows down and stops. The HUD crosshair
   locks onto the human bounding box. The radar mini-map in the corner shows amber
   and green blip dots. The background scanlines and grid overlay remain visible.
   Duration: 8-10 seconds. Resolution: 1920x1080. Style: same as patrol clip,
   consistent color grade, smooth transition from patrol motion to alert state.

   Preferred tool: Runway Gen-3 or Pika, with a still frame from the patrol clip
   as keyframe reference, or an image of the STASIS rover with a person in frame.


3. STASIS RESPONSE SCROLL
   Required filename: docs/assets/stasis-response-scroll.mp4

   Starts from the rover's stopped FPV after detection. A split-screen or overlay
   transition reveals a dark-themed browser dashboard on the operator's laptop
   screen showing the STASIS Mission Control interface. The dashboard displays:
   the live camera feed (matching the rover FPV), an alert card reading "human
   detected", the tracking map with the rover marker and an amber target dot, and
   command buttons. The operator's cursor clicks "STOP" and then "TRACK". The rover
   HUD updates to show "TRACKING ACTIVE" in amber. The video ends with the rover
   camera reframing to keep the human in the center of the frame. Duration: 8-10
   seconds. Resolution: 1920x1080. Style: consistent with previous clips, tactical
   dashboard aesthetic, dark theme with amber/green accent colors.

   Preferred tool: Runway Gen-3 or composite in video editor using the actual
   dashboard screenshot as overlay. Can be created as a screen recording of the
   real dashboard with a simulated detection, then composited with rover FPV.


4. STASIS LANDING PAGE HERO BACKGROUND
   Required filename: docs/assets/stasis-forest-real.jpg

   A dramatic wide-angle photograph or render of the STASIS rover on a forest
   floor, seen from a low angle. The rover is a small Raspberry Pi-based chassis
   with four wheels, a USB webcam on the front, wires visible in a clean technical
   way, and green LED indicators. Behind it, a blur of trees, ferns, and natural
   forest undergrowth creates a deep bokeh effect. Gentle god rays filter through
   the canopy. The image should feel like a field robotics expedition photo — not
   a product shot, but a capture from a real mission. Resolution: 2560x1440 or
   higher. Style: natural photography, earthy tones, mossy greens, amber sunlight,
   documentary feel, slight desaturation.

   Preferred tool: Midjourney or DALL-E 3 with a reference photo of the actual
   STASIS rover. If using Midjourney, add "--ar 16:9 --style raw" for
   photorealistic results.


5. STASIS BRAND LOGO
   Required filename: docs/assets/stasis-logo.png

   A minimalist circular emblem logo for the STASIS project. The design should
   feature a stylized radar/sonar sweep arc in amber/gold, a small rover/crosshair
   icon at center, and the word "STASIS" in a clean monospace or geometric
   sans-serif typeface around the outer ring. Dark background, 512x512px
   transparent PNG. Should work at 32x32px as a favicon-sized logo in the nav bar.
   Color palette: amber (#d7a34f), moss green (#9ac46b), and off-white (#f5f0e6)
   on dark (#030402).

   Preferred tool: Canva, Figma, or any SVG editor. Can also be generated with
   DALL-E 3 or Midjourney with "--no text" flag, then cleaned up manually.


=====================================================================
NOTES:
- All video files should be H.264 MP4, 1920x1080, 24-30fps, under 20MB each.
- The hero background (stasis-forest-real.jpg) is the most critical missing asset —
  the entire landing page hero section uses it.
- If video generation is not available, record actual rover footage and edit it.
- PNG logo should be 512x512px with transparent background.
- Put all assets in: docs/assets/
- Test files work with the site locally before deploying.
=====================================================================
